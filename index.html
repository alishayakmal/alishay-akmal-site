How a project runs

From messy to trusted

Audit

Trace where the data actually comes from, and where it breaks before it reaches anyone's screen.

Rebuild

Redesign the report or dashboard around the decision it's meant to support, not the data it happens to have.

Automate

Remove the manual steps that make a report late, wrong, or abandoned after month two.

Train

Hand it off with the context for people to trust it, extend it, and stop asking me what it means.

About

Beyond the tools

I'm drawn to the parts of analytics that don't show up on a resume: the messy handoff between teams, the report nobody trusts because nobody understands how it's built, the dashboard that gets opened once and never again.

My job is to fix that. I build systems people actually adopt, mentor analysts into people who can own a problem end to end, and keep translating between "the data says" and "here's what to do about it."

Outside of StackAdapt, I coach analysts and career switchers into their next role, and teach the dashboard-building process itself, since the best insight is worthless if nobody can read it.
